About Our Mission

Welcome to GYDES-MUN! We are an international organisation where young people (typically aged 11 to 25) step into the shoes of real-world diplomats. Instead of just reading about global issues, participants are assigned a country and roleplay as its representative. Over a multi-day conference, they debate pressing modern topics, negotiate deals with other student delegates, and work together to write solutions to major global problems. We organise Model United Nations Simulations for free of charge to provide experience in delegation, public speaking and International Relations every month! We believe that diplomacy should be accesible for all.

Why Participate?

Participating in Model UN simulations improves public speaking, critical thinking and confidence. Stepping up to the podium teaches you to conquer stage fright and deliver persuasive, impromptu speeches under pressure. Participating forces you out of your comfort zone, transforming how you communicate, think, and interact with the world.
